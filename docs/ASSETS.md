# Free art assets

Pixel-art packs found for Curation, saved in `assets/vendor/` (not shipped until
a piece is used in the game). Every pack here is free to use in a commercial
game. Credit is required unless the license is CC0, and pieces under CC-BY-SA
keep that license (edits to those images stay CC-BY-SA; the game itself is
unaffected). When a piece goes into the game, add its pack to the game's
credits screen. Each pack's own `Credits.txt` / `README.txt` (where it has one)
lists the original artists in full.

Rule of thumb: only pixel art in a 3/4 top-down view, close to our 32px grid.
No paid packs, no revenue share.

returned 403 (a bot check, so not tried further and not got around). Every licence below was read on
