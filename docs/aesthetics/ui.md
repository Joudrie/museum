# UI, menus, text and the builder app: findings (U-xx)

> Part of the aesthetics audit (29 Oct 2026, commit fa70331). Overview: [../AESTHETICS-AUDIT.md](../AESTHETICS-AUDIT.md). Visual version with every screenshot: https://claude.ai/artifact/5TdJk17Mx4g36tciuXPfvW (private to the owner).


Judged at 390x844 (deviceScaleFactor 2 for full screens, 3 for close crops), rich save, plus 360x640, large text,
left-handed and landscape. All shots are in `shots/ui/` (builder piece contact sheets in `shots/ui/pieces/`).
Scripts that produced them: `uiscripts/r1.cjs` … `r15.cjs` (run with `uiscripts/run.cjs`).
Note: the audit sandbox has no internet, so paintings loaded from wikimedia (e.g. The Horse Fair) show their placeholder.
That is still a real state (offline, slow 4G), so it is judged as one.

## 1. Typography (all screens)

- **[U-01] Pixelify Sans makes C read as O, 5 as S, Z as 2, B as G at the sizes used** (High) — `src/ui/tokens.css` `--ui-font`, `--fs-small/body/lead` (13/15/17px). At 3x crops: "Collect all" reads "Oollect all", "Curator's eye" "Ourator's eye", "Claim" "Olaim", "Carp" "Oarp", "Zara" "2ara", "Build" "Guild", "50" "S0", "Have 5 paintings" "Have S paintings", "5 of 5 meteorites" "S of S", milestone "25% of the codex" "2S%". In a money game where every number matters this is the worst readability flaw in the UI. The font is also drawn off its pixel grid (13/15/17px are not multiples of its design pixel), so strokes are anti-aliased to 1.5 px mush. → Pick a true bitmap pixel font with open C, flat-topped 5, slashed/clear Z and B (e.g. Daniel Linssen's m5x7 / m6x11, "Monogram", "Pixel Operator", "Silver"), render it only at integer multiples of its pixel size (e.g. 16/24/32 px) with `-webkit-font-smoothing: none`, and use a tabular-figure variant for numbers. Shots: [c-journal-goals.png](shots/ui/c-journal-goals.png), [c-hud-top-nohint.png](shots/ui/c-hud-top-nohint.png), [n-catch2.png](shots/ui/n-catch2.png), [r-convo-box.png](shots/ui/r-convo-box.png)
- **[U-02] Four font families on screen, not two** (High) — tokens say Pixelify + Georgia only, but: `ui-monospace` (DejaVu/SF Mono) in the income list right column "+16/hr · 2 paintings" (`src/style.css` `.ir-gold` l.1679, `.income-top b` l.1627), slider values `.slider b` (l.1509), staff bubble name `.talk-who` (l.556), toolkit meta `.ti-meta` (l.948), rating row `.rating-row b` (l.1183), `.hud-stats` (l.843) and `.pixel-btn` (l.271) fall back to monospace before skin.css overrides; `system-ui` sans in the sail/lead/boat/expedition UI (`.sail-dest` l.2298, `.lead-card` l.2482, `.bs-line`/`.bs-tap` l.2619/2631, `#exp-hud` l.2453, `#back-builder` l.2264); Georgia via `font: inherit` in the metal-detector badge and the rotate screen; plain `sans-serif` in the crash screen (index.html inline). → Delete every one-off `font-family` in style.css; chrome = the pixel font, museum prose = one serif, nothing else. Shots: [a02-hud-income.png](shots/ui/a02-hud-income.png), [n-leads.png](shots/ui/n-leads.png), [i-boat.png](shots/ui/i-boat.png)
- **[U-03] Serif dialogue inside pixel chrome** (Med) — conversation box (`.talk-text`, `.chat-text` in `src/ui/skin.css` l.20-26) puts a 17px Georgia line under a Pixelify name and role; the staff bubble is all-serif with a monospace name. UI.md says Georgia is for *museum prose*; people talking are not museum prose. → Dialogue in the UI pixel font (larger, 18-20px, 1.4 line height); keep serif only for painting blurbs, plaques and the viewer card. Shots: [r-convo-box.png](shots/ui/r-convo-box.png), [k-staff-bubble-crop.png](shots/ui/k-staff-bubble-crop.png)
- **[U-04] Faux italic on a pixel font** (Med) — artist lines, rarity lines and room subtitles ("Pieter Bruegel the Elder · 1565", "Common · ×1 held", "Entrance · Fixed") are Pixelify slanted by the browser: jagged, lower contrast, and on a pixel grid italics look broken. Seen in Wall spot, every collection grid, Storage, Shop, Rooms tab, Display menu. → No italics in pixel type; use the muted colour or a smaller size for secondary lines. Shots: [a10-wall-spot.png](shots/ui/a10-wall-spot.png), [a06-customize-rooms.png](shots/ui/a06-customize-rooms.png)
- **[U-05] ALL CAPS labels everywhere despite "never ALL CAPS"** (Low) — every section label is letter-spaced caps (NEXT GOALS, COLLECTIONS, THEME (ONE TAP), HAIRSTYLE, FLOOR PATTERN, TESTING (FOR NOW)), staff bubble "EDMUND CARETAKER" and "TAP TO CLOSE", income "FULL, collect!", the room plaque text `text.toUpperCase()` in `RoomScene.js` l.570. Two label tiers look identical ("LOOK" directly over "LIGHTING" in Settings). → Section labels in sentence case, gold or muted, one size step smaller than row titles; one label level per group. Shots: [a08-settings.png](shots/ui/a08-settings.png)
- **[U-06] Inconsistent title case** (Low) — "Curator's Office", "Restoration Studio", "Gallery Shop", "Your Museum" (strip) vs "Staff board", "Wall spot", "Your museum" (sheet), "New room". → Sentence case for every title.
- **[U-07] Long text squeezed into thin columns** (Med) — Staff board rows put a fixed-width button beside the description, so "Better mops" wraps to 7 lines of 2-3 words and "Better beans" to 5 (worse with large text: the title itself wraps). Toasts have a ~230px max width so "Main Island · walk back into the doors to go inside" is 3 lines and "Island One · the museum here is shut: sail home from the dock" is 4. → Stack the button under the text when the description is longer than one line; toasts up to the full safe width minus 32px, 1-2 lines max. Shots: [a12-staff-board.png](shots/ui/a12-staff-board.png), [b-large-staff.png](shots/ui/b-large-staff.png), [i-main-arrive.png](shots/ui/i-main-arrive.png)
- **[U-08] Toolkit card names clipped mid-word** (Med) — "Butterfly case", "Specimen shelf", "Gallery bench" wrap to 2 lines inside fixed-height cards and the second line is cut by the card bottom. `src/toolkit.js` `.ti-*` cards. → Fixed 2-line name area with ellipsis, or shorter display names. Shots: [b-toolkit-5.png](shots/ui/b-toolkit-5.png), [montage-m25.png](shots/ui/montage-m25.png)

## 2. Colour, frames and shape language (all screens)

- **[U-09] Two corner languages fight: smooth rounded cards vs square pixel buttons** (High) — sheets (14px radius), cards/rows (8px radius), filter chips (fully round pills), progress bars (round), toggles, the conversation box and the catch card are smooth anti-aliased CSS curves; buttons, HUD and close buttons are square bevelled pixel blocks. In one Journal row you see a 1px-smooth rounded gold card border wrapped around a hard pixel "Claim" button. → One pixel frame: 9-slice pixel borders (2px dark outline, 1px inner highlight, stepped 2-3 px corners) for sheets, cards, chips, bars and dialogs, drawn at integer scale. `--ui-r`, `--ui-r-sheet` in tokens.css go to 0 and the corner is in the frame art. Shots: [c-journal-goals.png](shots/ui/c-journal-goals.png), [c-journal-ships.png](shots/ui/c-journal-ships.png)
- **[U-10] Vector-smooth widgets inside a pixel game** (Med) — coin icon is a CSS radial gradient sphere (`.coin`, style.css l.852), notification dots are smooth pink circles with a glow pulse (`.badge` l.1851), the time-of-day dot is a blurred glowing orb (`.hud-time` l.1547), hair/wall colour swatches are perfect circles, sliders are browser range inputs with round thumbs, the daily wheel is a smooth vector pie, progress bars are pills. → Pixel versions of each: an 8x8 pixel coin with 1px outline and highlight, a 6x6 pixel "!" pip, pixel sun/moon icons, square pixel swatches with a 2px selected frame, a stepped pixel slider track with a square knob, a pixel-drawn wheel.
- **[U-11] Two progress-bar styles** (Low) — Journal overview uses green segmented pixel bars (good), the Codex list, goal cards, set plaques and the room-rating breakdown use smooth gold pills. → The segmented pixel bar everywhere (green when complete, gold while filling).
- **[U-12] Three "new" badge styles** (Med) — gold rectangle "8 new" on overview cards (`.jbadge`), orange-red rounded "NEW" stamp on every collection tile (`src/collections/tab.js`), red tilted "NEW" ribbon on toolkit cards, plus red dots on HUD buttons and toolkit category chips. Every tile in a freshly-filled collection shows a "NEW" stamp, so the grid is a wall of orange. → One "new" marker: a small gold pixel pip in the tile corner, plus the count on the overview card; red reserved for "needs you now".
- **[U-13] Primary-button weight is not hierarchy** (Med) — Ask dialogs show "Not now" and "Open it" both as full gold primaries (also "Not now" / "Buy dummy cameras"); Today shows its two main actions ("Collect 50 gold", "Spin") as quiet brown secondaries; Restoration Studio's "← Back to the bench" is a gold primary at the top. → Exactly one gold primary per screen, at the bottom, for the thing the player came to do; "Not now" is quiet/secondary. Shots: [a14-ask-unlock.png](shots/ui/a14-ask-unlock.png), [k-guard.png](shots/ui/k-guard.png), [a15-daily.png](shots/ui/a15-daily.png), [q-studio-piece.png](shots/ui/q-studio-piece.png)
- **[U-14] Disabled buttons look like errors or ghosts** (Med) — "Build a coffee counter" is a washed beige slab, "Owned" in the shop is grey, Rooms-tab up/down arrows and "Go" on the current room are near-invisible outlines, locked toolkit items fade to 40%. (Still stands from UI-AUDIT #12.) → Locked state = normal button frame with a lock icon and the reason as its label ("Needs a coffee counter"), muted ink, no opacity tricks.
- **[U-15] Low-contrast muted text on brown** (Med) — `--ui-muted` #b9a98f at 13px on `--ui-panel` is fine, but several places go darker: "Locked" rows in Income (grey on brown), visitor "The main hall · Rare" captions over black silhouettes, the builder's disabled area-bar buttons, the shop "Owned" buttons, toolkit locked cards "In winter". → Keep every text ≥ 4.5:1; locked rows keep full-contrast titles and put the lock in an icon.

## Systemic issues
- **Two visual languages on every screen:** pixel-bevel buttons and HUD vs smooth CSS (rounded cards, pills, gradient coins, glow dots, range sliders, vector wheel). The pixel half looks like the game; the CSS half looks like a web app.
- **Font sprawl:** Pixelify (ambiguous glyphs, off-grid sizes) + Georgia + system monospace + system-ui sans + browser default, chosen per screen rather than by role.
- **Glyphs as icons:** emoji and unicode symbols (★☆✓→↑↓▶←☰↶↷⟳📡🔒 and the collection emoji) stand in for icons, rendered by whatever system font has them.
- **Two icon sets** (src/icons.js 11x11 masks for Interact, src/ui/icons.js for everything else) with different grids, stroke weights and frames, and several icons whose metaphor reads wrong.
- **No item-scale rule:** sprites appear at 2x, 3x, 6x, 10x or fractional scale depending on the screen; tiny items float in big empty boxes, big ones overflow (stamps, toolkit).
- **Overlay stacking is unmanaged:** hint, toast, Warm badge, notification dots and sheets overlap the HUD, the art and each other; z-order puts tips above full-screen views.
- **Off-style "side" screens:** boat, crash, rotate, letters, dig/studio work surfaces and the share card were built outside the system and show it.
- **Builder pieces were sliced, not curated:** whole LPC sheets cut into cells with no check for completeness, scale, duplicates or naming, at a scale twice the game's characters.
