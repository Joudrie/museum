# People: art-direction audit (findings P-01 ...)

> Part of the aesthetics audit (29 Oct 2026, commit fa70331). Overview: [../AESTHETICS-AUDIT.md](../AESTHETICS-AUDIT.md). Visual version with every screenshot: https://claude.ai/artifact/5TdJk17Mx4g36tciuXPfvW (private to the owner).


Scope: every character part in `src/art.js` (`makeCharacter`, `withLooks`, `hairStyle`, `outfit`, `accessory`, `stepFrames`, HATS / HAIRSTYLES / OUTFITS / ACCESSORIES / FACIAL / GLASSES), all 11 baked frames (down, down_1, down_2, side, side_1, side_2, up, up_1, up_2, phone, sit), skin and hair palettes, visitor looks (`visitorLook` in `src/RoomScene.js`), staff, the 19 famous visitors (`src/famous.js`), emotes / bubbles / markers, portraits (conversation box, Customize turntable, journal cards), and people in the world (rooms, break room, Main Island, iso room).

Method: every part was baked through the live game's `window.__makeCharacter`, dumped at native size (`shots/people/raw/`, 1,300+ PNGs) and recomposed at 6-14x. Then the same people were checked in the running game at 390x844 (dpr 3, and dpr 2 for scaling) in the entrance, main hall, atrium, break room, Main Island and the iso room.
Shots are in `docs/aesthetics/shots/people/`; file names below link to them.

Sprite basics, so the findings make sense: one body, 20x27 px inside a 32x32 cell, drawn from three string tables (`FRONT`, `BACK`, `SIDE`, art.js ~1552). The palette letters are s/S skin, h/H hair, c/C/d jacket, w shirt, g tie, p pants, b shoes, o outline (#2a1a14), e eyes. In rooms and on islands every person is drawn at `setScale(1.15)` (`PLAYER_SCALE`, RoomScene.js:89, IslandScene.js:57). Kids are drawn at 1.15 x 0.85.

## A. Base body: head, face, anatomy

- **[P-01] The face is blank: two 1x2 eye dashes, no mouth, nose, brows or ears** (High). `FRONT` rows 4-10 (art.js ~1552). The 10 px face carries only `e` pixels at columns 3 and 8 of rows 6-7. There is no mouth, nose, eyebrows, cheek shading or eye highlight, so the face can show no emotion. Anything that covers the eyes (glasses, unibrow) or the lower face (moustache) takes over the whole face (see P-20, P-21, P-40). → Redraw the head with a 2x2 eye (dark pupil plus a 1 px light catch-light, or a 1 px white above it), a 1 px brow row, a 2 px mouth in a darker skin tone, 1 px ear bumps on the head outline at eye level, and a nose shadow pixel in side view. Add 3 expression variants (neutral, happy-closed, surprised). Shot: [faces-misc-14x.png](shots/people/faces-misc-14x.png), [poses-8x.png](shots/people/poses-8x.png).
- **[P-02] Square "Lego" head** (Med). The head steps from an 8 px top row to 10 to 12 with single-pixel corners (`'oooooooo','ohhhhhhhho','ohhHHhhhhhho'`) and is just as square at the chin, so at phone size every head is a box. → A rounder skull: a 2-px step at the crown corners, a 1-px cheek taper at rows 8-9 and a narrower chin, so the head reads as an oval. Kids get a rounder, bigger head.
- **[P-03] No ears anywhere** (Med). The front head sides are hair, then skin, then outline. There is no ear bump, so glasses have nowhere to hook, earrings (`look.earrings`, art.js ~1775) end up as yellow dots on the cheek, and the guard's earpiece (`look.earpiece`, art.js:1780) is a 1x2 black dot on the cheek edge. → A 1x2 skin-coloured ear bump outside the face outline at rows 6-7 (front and back views), and a 2x2 ear in side view. Earrings and the earpiece then attach to the ear lobe.
- **[P-04] The side-view jaw band reads as stubble on everyone** (Med). In `SIDE`, rows 9-10 are `'ooSsssssSo','ooSSSSSSoo'`: a full row of the darker `S` skin under the jaw. On light skin this reads as a goatee or five-o'clock shadow on every side-facing woman, kid and elder. Front rows 9-10 add `S` at both jaw corners, symmetric, which ignores the upper-left light. → Shade only the cheek away from the light and the underside of the chin (1-2 px), never a full row.
- **[P-05] The side-view nose is a 2x2 outline lump at eye height** (Med). `SIDE` rows 6-7 end `'...esoo'`: the profile bump is two outline pixels on the same rows as the eye, so the profile shows two dark marks side by side (eye plus nose). → Put the nose 1 px below the eye, as a skin pixel with a 1 px outline tip, and the mouth 2 px lower.
- **[P-06] The hair highlight is a flat 2x1 grey bar in the same spot on every head** (Med). `FRONT` row 2 `'ohhHHhhhhhho'`, `BACK` row 5 has a single `H` in the centre. On black hair (#1e1410, #2b1a10) `H` = mix with white 0.22 = #4c4440, a grey patch that reads as a bandage or a bald spot. The single centre pixel on every back view reads as a stray pixel or crown bald spot. → Hand-placed highlight clusters that follow the hairstyle's flow (an arc of 3-5 px along the crown, top-left), using a hue-shifted lighter tone (warmer for brown, bluer for black), never mixed toward grey.
- **[P-07] A long torso on stubby legs, no waist, no shoulders** (Med). Torso rows 12-20 are a 14 px-wide rectangle, 9 rows tall, with no taper, waist or shoulder slope. Legs are 4 rows (about 19% of height). At phone size people read as boxes on stumps, and dresses and gowns can't flare (see P-50). → Rebalance to roughly head 11 / torso 7 / legs 6 with a 1 px waist taper and sloped shoulders, in 3 builds (slim, standard, broad) plus kid and elder, as the ART-BIBLE asks.
- **[P-08] Arms are not drawn: one shaded column on each torso edge** (Med). The arms are just the `C` (light) left column and the `d` (dark) right column of the torso (`'oCccc...cccdo'`). There is no armpit gap or sleeve edge, so no arm reads and nothing can be held. → 2-3 px-wide arms separated from the torso by a 1 px darker crease or a background gap at the elbow, with a sleeve hem where a tee or short sleeve ends.
- **[P-09] Hands are single loose skin pixels at hip height** (Med). `FRONT` rows 18-19 put an `s` in column 1 and len-2 (`'osCccccccccdso'`). On light skin it is a stray orange dot. On dark skin (#583420) it disappears into the outline. → 2x2 hands at the end of the arm, with a 1 px darker underside and an outline.
- **[P-10] The feet sit on a black plank** (Med). Front: shoes `'obbbboobbbbo'` plus a bottom outline `'oooooooooooo'` that runs under both feet, so the two near-black shoes (#1a1418) and the outline merge into one 12x2 black slab, like a toy figure's base. Side: `'obbbbbbbbbbo'` is a 12 px single-colour slab sticking out both sides (a skateboard or ski). → Two separate shoes with a gap between them in front view, and a 1 px highlight on the toe. In side view the foot points forward (toe 3 px ahead, heel 1 px behind), with the sole a shade darker than the upper.
- **[P-11] Near-black outline on everything, against the ART-BIBLE's selective outline rule** (Med). Every edge, including the face and the inner leg gap, uses `o` = #2a1a14. With near-black shoes, black hair, black suits (guard, Lincoln, Seurat) and dark skin, the sprite becomes one dark mass (P-62). The bible says "selective, colour-matched dark outline ... not black". → Outline each region in a darker shade of its own ramp (dark brown on skin, deep navy on a blue coat), keep near-black only at the outer silhouette's bottom, and drop interior outlines inside the face.
- **[P-12] Pure white `Q` pixels break the "no pure white" rule** (Low). `LOOK_PAL.Q = '#ffffff'` is used for the bald-head shine, the shades glint, the camera lens and the phone screen. They are the brightest pixels on screen and pop like dead pixels. → Use a warm cream (#fff3d6) or a pale tint of the material.
- **[P-13] The light direction is half applied** (Low). The torso is lit from the left (light `C` column left, dark `d` right) and the hair highlight is top-left, but the face, legs, shoes and every hat are flat with symmetric jaw shading. Mirrored side views (`setFlipX`) also flip the light to the right. → Shade hats, legs and the face from the upper left too. For flipped side frames either accept it or bake a true left view.

## B. Views and animation

- **[P-14] The side walk has one frame: `side_1` and `side_2` are identical** (High). `stepFrames` returns `[out(a), out(a)]` for the side view (art.js:1974; the PNGs are byte-identical). `animateWalker` (motion.js) cycles stand, step, stand, step, so a side walk is a 2-pose scissor that always has the same leg forward. → A real 4-frame side cycle (contact, pass, contact with the other leg, pass) with an opposite arm swing and a 1 px body bob on the pass frames.
- **[P-15] The front and back walks only lift a foot 1 px** (Med). The down and up step frames shift one leg's pants and shoe up one row and delete its outline row (art.js ~1976-1986). There is no arm swing, no head or hair bob and no torso shift. At phone size people glide. The only bob is `setOrigin(0.5, 1 + 1/32)`, which moves the whole sprite 1 px. → 4-frame front and back walks with legs alternating 2 px, arms swinging 1 px, and hair or ponytail lagging 1 frame.
- **[P-16] The side-view stride is just a dark notch between the legs** (Med). `stepFrames` side cuts two outline pixels into the middle of the pants rows and lifts the back of the shoe slab. The silhouette barely changes, and the shoe plank tilts like a see-saw. Shot: [walk-legs-12x.png](shots/people/walk-legs-12x.png). → Draw the stride: front leg forward 2 px, back leg back 2 px, back heel lifted, knee bend 1 px.
- **[P-17] The side view's arm is at the back edge and its hand is a square in mid-torso** (High). `SIDE` rows 13-17 have the light `C` arm column on the far back edge. Rows 18-19 `'oCccssccdo'` put a 2x2 skin square in the middle of the body, not touching the arm. At phone size it reads as a pocket or a patch on the belly. The jagged back contour (row lengths 9 to 10 to 11 centred differently) also pushes a light-blue `C` pixel outside the outline at the elbow. Shots: [customize-preview-crop.png](shots/people/customize-preview-crop.png), [hair-a-8x.png](shots/people/hair-a-8x.png) (side column). → Draw the side arm as its own 3 px limb hanging from the shoulder slightly in front of the body midline, with the hand at its end and a straight outlined back contour.
- **[P-18] Back views have no back-only detail and keep the jacket's front seam** (Low). `BACK` torso `'oCccccdcccccdo'` shows a centre `d` seam and a second dark column on the right edge, so the back looks like a front with the face removed. Accessories vanish in back views: bow tie, necklace, badge and the camera strap are all front-only, and the scarf is 1 px (`accessory`, art.js ~1695). → Back views get shoulder blades or a collar line, apron ties, bag body and strap, scarf knot or tail, and the hood shape.
- **[P-19] The sit pose is the front view with the legs cut off** (High). `makeCharacter` sit (art.js:1940) slices out 4 leg rows and keeps one pants row plus the shoes, so a seated person is a stubby, legless standing figure, the same height as standing minus 2 px. There are no thighs, knees or bent pose, and the arms stay at the sides. On a bench the bench plank shows at chest height behind them. Shots: [sit-bench.png](shots/people/sit-bench.png), [sit-sofa.png](shots/people/sit-sofa.png), [phone-sit-12x.png](shots/people/phone-sit-12x.png). → A true seated sprite: thighs foreshortened forward (2-3 px of lap in pants colour), knees, shins hanging in front of the seat edge, hands on the lap, and the body 4-5 px lower. Add a side-seated variant for benches seen from the side.
- **[P-20] The phone pose is a black button on the belly with four hands** (High). `look.phone` (art.js:1847) paints a 2x2 `k` block with a white `Q` pixel at rows 16-17 (navel height) and adds two skin pixels below it at row 18. The original hands at the sides stay, so the sprite has four hands, and the phone reads as a belt buckle or a hole in the stomach. Shot: [phone-sit-12x.png](shots/people/phone-sit-12x.png). → Bend both forearms in to chest height, put a 2x3 phone with a 1 px lit screen in the hands, and tilt the head down 1 px. Optionally add a faint screen glow on the face at night.
- **[P-21] No idle, talk or job poses at all** (Med). Every standing person is a frozen frame, and the ART-BIBLE's 2-frame idle is missing. Staff never mop, sweep or point (the janitor's mop is a separate prop leaning beside them). → A 2-frame breathing idle (1 px shoulder rise every ~1 s), a talk pose, and the job poses from PEOPLE-SPRITES.md section 3.9.
- **[P-22] The iso room uses the top-down sprite and a different scale** (Low). `IsoScene` draws `player_*` at scale 1.0 (IsoScene.js:21) against 1.15 elsewhere, and walks diagonally using only the 4 orthogonal facings. Shot: [iso-player.png](shots/people/iso-player.png). → Either use the same scale everywhere, or bake 4 diagonal facings (three-quarter front and back) for the angled room.

## C. Glasses and face marks

- **[P-23] Round glasses are two solid 3x3 black squares: everyone wearing them looks like a skull** (High). `glasses === 'round'` (art.js:1743) turns the skin either side of each eye, and the row above it, into `k` (#1d1714). Each eye becomes a solid 3x3 near-black block. There is no lens, no frame-only ring, no bridge and no arms. Glasses are given to the caretaker Edmund, every nerd, 70% of historians, 50% of elders, 60% of teachers and 20% of casuals (`visitorLook`). The caretaker behind the ticket desk (white hair, pale skin, two black sockets) reads as a skeleton at phone size. This is worse than the sunglasses the user flagged. Shots: [round-glasses-skull-14x.png](shots/people/round-glasses-skull-14x.png), [entrance-people-row.png](shots/people/entrance-people-row.png), [desk-crowd.png](shots/people/desk-crowd.png). → A 1 px dark frame ring round each eye with the skin and the eye visible inside (or a pale grey-blue lens tint), a 1 px bridge pixel over the nose, a 1 px arm from each lens to the ear (P-03), and in side view one lens ring plus an arm running back to the ear.
- **[P-24] Sunglasses read as a black blindfold, and in side view the glint becomes a white eyeball** (High). `glasses === 'shades'` (art.js:1752) fills row 6 solid from one eye to the other, plus 3 px under each eye on row 7, all in the same near-black as the outline. The single white `Q` glint sits on the left eye. There is no frame or lens split, no arms to the ears, and on dark skin with black hair (the guard) the whole upper face is black. In side view the eye pixel itself becomes the white glint, so the guard seems to stare with one white eye. Shot: [guard-glasses-14x.png](shots/people/guard-glasses-14x.png), [hall-guard-column.png](shots/people/hall-guard-column.png). → Two separate dark lenses in charcoal blue (#2c3442), each with a 1 px lighter rim on top, a 1 px bridge, 1 px temple arms back to the ears, and a 1 px cream glint in the upper-left corner of each lens. In side view, one lens in front of the eye, the arm back to the ear, and the glint on the lens, never on the eye spot.
- **[P-25] Influencers get skull glasses, not the intended sunglasses** (Med). `if (kind === 'influencer') look.glasses = r(10) < 0.5; // sunglasses indoors` (RoomScene.js:165) sets `true`, which `withLooks` maps to `'round'`. → Set `'shades'`, once P-24's shades are redrawn.
- **[P-26] The unibrow is an 8 px black bar, so Frida looks like she wears a visor** (Med). `look.unibrow` (art.js:1762) paints row 5 from one eye to the other in `e`. With black hair (#1e1410) the brow joins the hairline into one black band. In side view the row-index maths uses rows of different lengths, so the brow is shifted or missing. Shot: [faces-misc-14x.png](shots/people/faces-misc-14x.png). → A 1 px brow in hair colour, slightly arched (raised 1 px over each eye and dipping at the centre), with a gap of skin above it.
- **[P-27] Freckles read as a grubby moustache shadow** (Low). `look.freckles` puts 6 `Z` pixels (skin times 0.72) in a 2-row cluster across rows 8-9. They are low contrast and sit where a moustache would. They are front view only. → 3-4 single, spaced pixels high on the cheeks (row 7-8), one or two on the side view cheek.
- **[P-28] The earring is a yellow dot on the jaw and the earpiece is a mole** (Low). `look.earrings` sets `Y` at row 9 inside the face edge. `look.earpiece` sets `k` at rows 7-8 column 1, invisible on the guard's dark skin and a dirt speck on light skin. → Attach both to an ear (P-03), and give the earpiece a 1 px curly cable down to the collar.
- **[P-29] Eye colour choices are invisible** (Low). Customize offers 6 eye colours (`CHARACTER_PALETTES.e`, decor.js:17), but the eye is 2 dark pixels and blue #3a5a8a on skin barely shows. → With the 2x2 eye of P-01, the iris colour becomes 1 px under a dark pupil pixel.

## D. Hairstyles (`HAIRSTYLES`, `hairStyle()`, art.js ~1593)

- **[P-30] "Short" is identical to the base sprite, and every style shares one crown** (Med). `short` isn't handled, and 9 of 13 styles change only a few pixels of the same short-hair cap. So silhouettes barely differ at 20 px tall, and kinds can't be read by hair (Pokémon's "silhouette first" rule). → Each style owns its outline shape (crown height, side volume, fringe line).
- **[P-31] The side part is a tan scar on top of the head** (Low). `part` sets `S` (shaded skin) at rows 1-2 column 4, so the part line is a tan stripe through the hair. It shows only in front view; side and back are identical to short. → Mark the part with a 1 px darker hair line and a swept fringe shape, with the sweep visible in side and back views.
- **[P-32] The buzz cut is a waffle or hairnet checkerboard** (Med). `buzz` dithers h/H in a strict `(r + i) % 2` checker over the whole cap, and over the entire back of the head in the back view. It keeps the full short-hair volume. Shot: [hair-a-8x.png](shots/people/hair-a-8x.png). → A tight cap 1 px inside the skull outline, skin visible at the temples and nape, a flat single tone with a sparse 2-3 px highlight. No checker (the bible allows dithering only on big fabric and stone).
- **[P-33] Curly hair is diagonal speckle noise** (Med). `curly` widens rows 1-3 by one column and paints `H` where `(r*2 + i) % 3 === 0`, which gives diagonal streaks of light dots, not curls. → Curls as round 2-3 px clumps with a dark crease under each, and a scalloped outline (bumps every 2-3 px along the silhouette).
- **[P-34] The afro is only 2 px bigger than short hair and has a black line through it** (High). `afro` adds 2 `pre` rows and widens rows 0-8 by one column. Widening row 0, which is all outline `'oooooooo'`, leaves that outline row inside the hair as a dark seam across the crown. The speckle `H` pattern matches the curly one. It reads as "curly with a line", never as an afro. Shot: [hair-b-8x.png](shots/people/hair-b-8x.png). → A round silhouette about 16 px wide by 12 px tall (1.4x the head), a clean single outline, a soft textured edge (bumps), a 2-tone volume with an upper-left highlight arc, and a hairline at the forehead.
- **[P-35] Pigtails are ear-muffs** (Med). `pigtails` widens rows 5-9 by one column (a `U` tie at row 5), so they stick out 1-2 px at ear height and stop at the jaw. They are invisible in side view (1 px at column 1). On kids they read as headphones or monkey ears ([sit-stool.png](shots/people/sit-stool.png)). → Two bunches that hang below the jaw to the shoulders (3x5 px each) with a coloured tie, visible behind the head in side view.
- **[P-36] The ponytail is invisible from the front, and the tie pixels look like ear studs** (Low). The front view only sets two `U` (dark brown) pixels at row 3 on either side of the head. Back view: a 2 px stick down to row 13 with stripes. → Show the tail's tip peeking past the neck on one side in front view, give it a tapered 3 px-wide tail with a coloured scrunchie in back and side views, and swing it 1 px in walk frames.
- **[P-37] The bob draws an unexplained dark headband on every bob** (Med). `look.hair === 'bob'` (art.js:1831) replaces row 2 with `'oUUUUUUUUUUo'`. That band is Cleopatra's gold diadem (its comment says so), but every kid or influencer with a bob gets a `U` = #5a3b2c brown stripe across the head. Shot: [hair-b-8x.png](shots/people/hair-b-8x.png), [combos-b-8x.png](shots/people/combos-b-8x.png) (dress-scarf). → Make the band a separate accessory (diadem or headband). The bob itself is straight bangs at brow level and a flat-ended curtain to the jaw in all three views.
- **[P-38] The mohawk leaves tufts at the temples and turns the side view into a flat cap** (Med). Front: only rows 1-3 are cleared to skin, so the `h` pixels at rows 4-5 stay as dark side tufts by the eyes. Side: only row 1 keeps hair and rows 2-8 become skin, so the profile is a bald head with a 2-row flat "lid", like a beret. Back: a 2 px stripe running into the nape. → A tall 3 px-wide crest (4-5 rows above the skull) with spiky edges, a shaved skull in a slightly darker skin tone, and the crest drawn front to back in side view.
- **[P-39] Wild hair is a flat shelf with a fence of dark teeth on top** (Med). `look.hair === 'wild'` (art.js:1825) forces rows 1-2 to a 14 px slab, wider than the head, and adds `pre` rows `'o.o..o.o' / 'ohoohhooho'`: single dark outline pixels with gaps. At phone size it reads as a crown or a flat hat. Einstein, the only user, looks crowned ([famous-c-8x.png](shots/people/famous-c-8x.png)). → Irregular 2-3 px tufts bursting sideways and up, wider at the temples than the crown (Einstein's shape), with a single continuous outline and no floating single pixels.
- **[P-40] Bald heads keep dark hair pixels at the crown corners, and the back view reads as a face** (Low). `look.bald` clears from column 2 to len-3, so row 1 keeps `h` at both ends: two dark specks on top of the dome. In back view the skin dome over a horseshoe of hair (rows 7-8) looks like a forehead over a beard, so bald men (Klimt, Socrates, Shakespeare, grumps) seem to face both ways. Shot: [famous-b-8x.png](shots/people/famous-b-8x.png) (up column). → A clean dome with an upper-left highlight cluster (2-3 px, cream not white), a fringe that wraps around the back at ear level, and a darker nape so the back view reads as a back.
- **[P-41] "Long" hair stops at the shoulder** (Low). `long` ends at row 11-12, the collar, in every view. → Hair to mid-back in back view (rows 12-16) and over one shoulder in front view, swaying 1 px in walk frames.

## E. Hats (`HATS`, `withLooks`, art.js ~1855)

- **[P-42] `look.hat = true` always means a SUN HAT: artists, janitors and random visitors get the wrong hat** (High). `const hat = look.hat === true ? 'sun' : look.hat` (art.js:1855). In `visitorLook`, the artist's "beret" (RoomScene.js:161, comment `// a beret`), the janitor's cap (RoomScene.js:170) and the 15% casual/couple/fanboy hat all pass `true`. So every janitor wears a wide-brim slate sun hat and reads as a fisherman or ranger, and artists wear red or black sun hats. Shot: [break-staff-row.png](shots/people/break-staff-row.png), [staff-8x.png](shots/people/staff-8x.png). → Pass the hat id: artist `'beret'`, janitor `'cap'`, casual a mix of cap, beanie and bucket hat.
- **[P-43] Hair `pre` rows poke through hats: a bun, afro, mohawk or wild hair sits ON TOP of the hat** (High). Hats that don't set their own `pre` (sun, cap, crown, laurel) leave the hairstyle's `pre` rows in place, so the bun knob, the afro's 2 crown rows and the mohawk spike stack above the brim. The hat looks worn under the hair. In-game this hits casual visitors with an afro or bun who roll a hat. Shot: [hatclip-8x.png](shots/people/hatclip-8x.png), [combos-a-8x.png](shots/people/combos-a-8x.png) (mohawk-cap). → Layering rule: a hat masks the hair above its band. Each hat defines which hair rows it hides, and big hair (afro, bun) gets a hat-compatible variant (an afro squashed under a beanie, a bun below a sun hat's brim at the back).
- **[P-44] Hats have no highlight, so dark hats melt into the outline** (High). `T` and `U` get no light or shadow shades (only `c` does, `makeCharacter`). A black top hat (#1e1e22 / #2a2a2e) next to the #2a1a14 outline becomes one black blob, and Rembrandt's beret is literally the outline colour (`T: '#2a1a14'`). Seurat, Lincoln, Vermeer, Napoleon and the gallery's black hats all lose their shape. Shots: [hats-a-8x.png](shots/people/hats-a-8x.png), [famous-a-8x.png](shots/people/famous-a-8x.png). → Derive `T` light and shadow shades the way `c` is derived. Give every hat a 1 px top-left rim highlight and a dark underside of the brim, and outline dark hats in a lighter ramp colour.
- **[P-45] The hat band and brim colour `U` is always brown, and players can't change it** (Med). `LOOK_PAL.U = '#5a3b2c'` is used for the cap visor in front view, the beanie cuff, the hard-hat brim, the straw band, the top-hat band and the bob band. Customize only offers `T` (decor.js:385). So a red cap has a brown visor, a blue beanie a brown cuff, and the cap's visor in front view reads as a fringe of hair. → Derive `U` from `T` (darker shade) by default and add a "band / trim" swatch row.
- **[P-46] The sun hat and straw hat are 1 px-thick plates floating on the hair** (Med). `sun`: the crown is just the existing head-top rows recoloured, and the brim is one 16 px row with outline only at its tips. `straw`: an 18 px 1 px line. They read as halos or frisbees, with no shadow on the face. → Brim 2 px thick (top tone and darker underside), 14-16 px wide, a 3-4 row crown with a band, a 1-row shadow of the brim on the forehead, and a slight elliptical droop at the ends. Add straw texture as a 1 px weave pattern on the straw hat only.

## Systemic issues

- **One body, one face, recoloured.** Every visitor, staff member, famous person and the player is the same 20x27 template, told apart only by palette and 1-2 px marks. Silhouettes don't differ, so kinds, staff and famous people don't read at phone size (P-30, P-83, L).
- **Details drawn as 1-2 px marks in outline-black.** Glasses, unibrow, moustache, camera, phone, badge, earpiece, scarf and bow tie are all 1-2 px clusters of near-black or a single colour, so they read as holes, mouths or dirt (P-23, P-24, P-26, P-54, P-69, P-70, P-20).
- **Row-relative string surgery.** Parts are painted by editing row strings by index, and rows of different lengths are centred differently, which causes misaligned features (side unibrow, moustache, topknot), outline breaks (the elbow pixel, the "!" bubble's right edge, the afro's internal seam) and parts left behind under hats (P-17, P-34, P-43, P-53, P-57, P-106). The redesign should author layers as absolute-coordinate overlays (or PNG layers via `public/swap/`).
- **Side and back views are afterthoughts.** Most accessories, hats and hair styles are front-only or identical in all views. The side view's arm, hand, feet and jaw are wrong, and the side walk has one frame (P-14, P-17, P-18, P-49, P-71).
- **Black-on-black and no hats shading.** A near-black outline plus near-black shoes, suits, hair and hats, with no `T` shading, turns dark-dressed people into blobs and hides most hats (P-11, P-44, P-62, P-74).
- **Style clashes around people.** Smooth vector shadows (island ellipse, turntable ellipse), radial-gradient blurs (`lt_shadow`), a system-font star glyph and monospace quips, and 1.15x non-integer scaling sit on top of a pixel-art world (P-105, P-111, P-115, P-119, P-120).
- **Placement ignores bodies.** Followers stack, staff stand inside furniture, visitors overlap and stand behind the ticket desk, and emotes use fixed offsets (P-82, P-85, P-109, P-116, P-118).

## Keep (already works)

- The size target (about 1 tile wide by 1.5 tiles tall) fits the 32px grid and the doors.
- The cap's side view (a visor jutting forward) and van Gogh's straw hat, ginger beard and blue palette: the best-reading pieces in the set.
- Depth sort by feet y works: people correctly pass behind fountains, desks and columns.
- The leash and dog companion concept, the couples and the school trips are good life, they just need spacing (P-85, P-118).
