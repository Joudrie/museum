# Creatures and collection-item art: audit findings

> Part of the aesthetics audit (29 Oct 2026, commit fa70331). Overview: [../AESTHETICS-AUDIT.md](../AESTHETICS-AUDIT.md). Visual version with every screenshot: https://claude.ai/artifact/5TdJk17Mx4g36tciuXPfvW (private to the owner).


Auditor domain: pets, island critters and wildlife, ambient and weather particles, fish (collection, fishing, tanks), every
code-drawn collection item (`src/collections/data/*.js` via `_px.js`), pieces shown on fixtures, and the item art in the dig and the
Restoration Studio.

How it was looked at: every sprite was pulled out of the real drawing code at 1x (an esbuild bundle of `src/pets.js`,
`src/critters.js`, `src/ambient.js` and `src/collections/*`), then enlarged 4-10x with nearest
neighbour. Each one was then checked in the running game at 390x844 (DPR 3): pets walking and sitting next to the curator,
the Island One pond and beach by day, at night, in rain and in snow, fishing, every Journal grid, the viewer, a room set up
with every fixture type, the dig and the Restoration Studio.

Scale reference: in game, 1 art pixel is about 1.35 CSS px on the phone. The curator is about 44 px tall.

---

## 1. Pets (`src/pets.js`: `quadruped()`, `parrot()`, `Pet`)

- **[C-01] Pets are about twice the size they should be, next to people and next to the island's own animals** (High). `quadruped()` draws on a 32x32 canvas: the cat is 22 px tall from the front, half the curator's 44 px height. The pet bunny is 27 px tall with its ears, while the island rabbit (`ambient.js rabbit_0`) is 11 px. The pet cat is twice as long as the napping cat on the same island, and the pet parrot (17 px) stands taller than the swan. On the phone a pet cat reads as a big dog or a fox, and a bunny reads as a sheep. → Cat and dog should be 14-16 px tall from the front (30-35% of a person), the bunny 12-14 px with its ears, the parrot 10-12 px. Use one drawing per species for both the pet and the wild animal, so a cat is the same size everywhere. Shots: [scale-pets-vs-wildlife-6x.png](shots/creatures/scale-pets-vs-wildlife-6x.png), [cat-sit-side-3x.png](shots/creatures/pets-game/cat-sit-side-3x.png), [parrot-bunny-3x.png](shots/creatures/pets-game/parrot-bunny-3x.png)
- **[C-02] The front view is a "snowman": two equal outlined blobs stacked, the same for every species** (High). `front()`: the body is `B(16,24,6,5)` and the head is `B(16,16,6,5)`, the same 13x11 ellipse twice, with no neck, shoulders or haunches. The front legs are 2x3 outline stubs hidden under the body. Cat, dog and bunny have exactly the same silhouette and differ only in their ears, so from the front they read as hamsters or teddy bears. → A smaller head overlapping a pear-shaped body, two visible front legs in fur colour with 1 px paws, a chest tuft, and a muzzle and head shape for each species (a wedge-shaped cat face, a longer dog snout, a round bunny face).
- **[C-03] Legs and tails are drawn in the outline colour, so the animals look like they wear black boots** (High). In `side()` and `front()` every leg is `R(x, 27, 2, 3, OUT)`, solid `#1d1714`. The cat tail is a chain of 2x2 `OUT` squares with one light pixel at the tip, so an orange cat has a black stick tail with an orange dot floating at its end (it reads as a candle wick or an antenna). The dog tail is two loose black 3x2 bars (`R(6,20,...)` and `R(4,18,...)`), and in `side_1` the upper bar floats free of the body. → Fill legs and tail with fur colour and put a 1 px outline around them. The cat tail should be a curved 2 px rope with a light tip and the dog tail a tapered wedge, both joined to the body.
- **[C-04] The dog's left ear covers its left eye, so every dog looks like it is winking** (High). In `ears()`, the dog ears `R(x-5, y-1, 3, 6, OUT)` and `R(x-4, y, 2, 5, dark)` are drawn after the eyes at `R(13,15,2,2)`. Pixel dump: the left eye has lost its x=13 column and its white catchlight, leaving a 1x2 black dash, while the right eye keeps its 2x2 with a catchlight. This happens in every dog colour, including the leashed dogs that visitors bring in. → Draw the ears before the eyes, and hang them outside the head (x 8-10 and 22-24), tapered, with a darker inner shade. Shot: [pet-dog-0-8x.png](shots/creatures/pet-dog-0-8x.png)
- **[C-05] Dog ears are square blocks at the head's top corners, so the head reads as a box, a helmet or headphones** (Med). They are 3x6 rectangles with no taper or droop, the same in the front, back and sitting views. → Floppy ears should be teardrop flaps that start at the top of the head and hang below the eye line. Pointed-ear colours (husky, shepherd) need upright triangles.
- **[C-06] The dog's side head shows both ears, plus a muzzle that floats** (Med). `side()` calls the same `ears()` with the head centre, so the side view has one dark strip at the back of the head and a black 3x6 bar across the face. The snout is a separate light blob `B(hx+5,hy+1,2,2)` with a 1 px nose at `hx+7,hy`, so the head reads as a box with an "E" on it. → The side view needs one ear, a wedge-shaped muzzle that is part of the head outline, an eye, and a nose at the tip of the snout.
- **[C-07] Cat ears are 3x3 black squares with one pink pixel, so they read as dots or horns, not ears** (Med). `ears()` for the cat: `R(x-3,y-3,3,3,OUT)` and `R(x+2,y-3,3,3,OUT)`. → 3-4 px triangles in fur colour with an outline and a pink inner pixel, tilted slightly outward.
- **[C-08] The black cat and black dog are shapeless dark blobs** (High). The coat is `#2a2420` (`PET_COLORS.cat[1]`, `dog[2]`) and the outline is `#1d1714`, about 4% apart in brightness, so the silhouette, legs, ears and eyes all disappear. The black cat shows only two white catchlight pixels, and the black dog shows only one, because of C-04. → Make black coats a blue-grey `#3a3848` with a `#5a586a` rim light on top, keep the outline darker, and give the black cat yellow-green eyes. Shots: [pet-cat-all-4x.png](shots/creatures/pet-cat-all-4x.png) (row 1), [pet-dog-all-4x.png](shots/creatures/pet-dog-all-4x.png) (row 2)
- **[C-09] Every pet has the same 2x2 black eyes with a white catchlight** (Med). Cats should have green or amber eyes with a slit. On the white bunny the white catchlight blends into the fur, so its eyes read as sleepy dashes. The island's sitting cat (`napcat_up`) already has green eyes, so the two cats on the same island don't match. → Coloured irises for each species (cat green or amber, dog dark brown, bunny dark red-brown), with one catchlight that stays readable on light fur.
- **[C-10] The back view has a "bald patch", and the tail is hidden behind the head** (Med). `front(…, back)` draws `E(16,15,3,2, shade(col,0.9))` on the back of the head, which reads as a hat, a bandage or a bald spot. The cat's back-view tail (y 16-22) is drawn before the head blob (y 11-21), so from behind you only see a black notch at the neck. The dog's tail stub `R(15,17,...)` is hidden the same way. → Draw the tail last, rising beside the body, and swap the patch for normal shading plus the backs of the ears.
- **[C-11] The walk cycle is two frames with a 1 px leg nudge, so pets glide** (Med). The front `_1` frame lifts one leg by 1 px. The side `_1` frame shifts the legs ±1 px with no body bob. Frames swap at 8 fps (`Pet.update`). → Four frames per direction (contact and pass), a 1 px body bob, tail sway, and the head leading.
- **[C-12] The bunny's hop frame has no legs** (Med). In `side(step)` for the bunny, the hop draws legs at `R(x,25,3,2)`, inside the body ellipse, so they are covered and the mid-hop bunny is a legless loaf sliding 2 px up. The front hop is just the standing pose raised 2 px over a shadow that doesn't move. → A real hop pose: hind legs stretched back, front paws tucked, ears swept back, and the shadow shrinking at the top of the arc.
- **[C-13] Sitting from the front looks exactly like standing, and there is no back-view sit** (Med). `front(false,false,true)` adds sit legs at y=28 that the body hides, so `down_sit` is pixel-identical to `down`. There is no `up_sit`, so `Pet.update` snaps a sitting pet to the front view. The side sit has a flat 6x4 rectangle for a belly that looks like a cushion. → A proper sit: haunches out, front legs straight, tail wrapped around the feet, and sits for both front and back.
- **[C-14] Bunny ears are rigid 3x9 parallel stalks, like antennae or a tuning fork** (Med). In `ears()` for the bunny, both ears are the same straight bar. The white belly `#ffffff` doesn't show on `#f4f0e6` fur, and the nose is a black 2x1 bar that reads as a mouth. → Tapered ears with a slight outward lean or one floppy ear, a pink inner strip, a pink Y-shaped nose, and a belly that is a separate cream or white tone.
- **[C-15] The parrot is an egg with a box beak, and its wings and tail are loose rectangles** (High). In `parrot()`, the beak is a 2x2 (front) or 2x3 (side) yellow block with no outline and no hook. The front wings are unoutlined 2x5 dark rectangles that stick out of the silhouette. The flap frame is a pair of horizontal black 5x3 bars, so the bird reads as a T-pose or a bow tie. In the side flap the wing is an outlined rectangle floating above the back. The tail is a flat unoutlined blue bar, and the feet are 1x2 orange sticks. → Make the beak hooked, dark grey or horn-coloured, and outlined, with a white eye ring. The wing should be an outlined teardrop on the body that spreads into primaries when flapping, with long two-tone tail feathers.
- **[C-16] The yellow parrot has no beak and the blue parrot has no tail** (Med). The beak is always `#f2c53d` and the tail always `#3a78c8`, so they vanish on the `#f2c53d` and `#3a78c8` body colours in `PET_COLORS.parrot`. → Set beak and tail colours per body colour (yellow body with a dark beak and a green tail, blue body with a yellow belly and a dark tail).
- **[C-17] The parrot "perches" at the curator's hip, not the shoulder, and has no shadow in flight** (Med). The perch target in `Pet.update` is `(o.x+5, o.y-19)`, and with origin (0.5,1) the parrot's feet end up 19 px above the ground, which is hip height on a 44 px curator (measured: player y 336, parrot y 317). Its depth is always `y+30`, so it is always drawn in front of the curator, even when it should be behind. The parrot canvas has no shadow ellipse, unlike the four-legged pets. → Perch at about y-34 with the feet gripping the shoulder, depth +1 only while perched, and a small shadow on the ground while flying.
- **[C-18] Pets park right behind the curator, so their head pokes up over the curator's head like a hat** (Med). When the curator stops after walking up or down, the pet stops at trail point `length-9`, on the same x and 9 steps back. A 22-27 px pet behind a 44 px person shows its ears and head above the hair. Shots: cat and bunny "sitting on" the curator in [cat-sit-side-3x.png](shots/creatures/pets-game/cat-sit-side-3x.png) and [parrot-bunny-3x.png](shots/creatures/pets-game/parrot-bunny-3x.png). → Park 12-16 px to one side, turn toward the curator, then sit.
- **[C-19] The colour options are near-duplicates and there are no markings** (Low). Dog `#a0703f` and `#6a4a2a` look almost the same at phone size. Every pet is one flat colour with no tabby stripes, tuxedo or calico patches, spots, or white socks and blaze. → Six or so readable coats per species, each with a marking layer.
- **[C-20] Builder-placed parrots hover frozen in the air** (Low). In `IslandScene.animal()`, `setOrigin(0.5, 1.5)` holds them 16 px off the ground at all times, idle uses the `side` frame with no flapping, and no shadow is drawn. → Perch on a fence, post or branch when idle, flap when moving, and draw a ground shadow.
- **[C-21] Visitor dog leashes share one depth and are smooth vector lines** (Low). In `RoomScene` (around line 2773), one `Graphics` object holds every leash, and `setDepth(...)` is set from the last NPC in the loop, so leashes draw over or under the wrong people. The leash is a 1 px anti-aliased `lineStyle` line starting at a fixed hand offset `±5,-12` that ignores the visitor's scale (kids and elders are scaled). → One leash per dog, drawn as pixels (Bresenham in a pixel texture) from a hand anchor the character art provides.

## 2. Island critters (`src/critters.js`: `critterTextures()`, `Critters`)

- **[C-22] The swan always swims backwards** (High). `updateSwan()` has `sw.a -= dt*0.12` and `setFlipX(Math.sin(sw.a) > 0)`. The x velocity follows +sin(a) and the sprite faces right, so the flip is the wrong way round: measured over 5 s, x went 199.4 → 198.0 (moving left) with flipX false (facing right). It glides tail-first all the time. → `setFlipX(Math.sin(sw.a) < 0)`.
- **[C-23] The jumping fish at sea leaps tail-first, every time** (Med). The `fish` texture (8x4) faces left (eye at x=1, tail block at x=6-7). `jump()` flips when `dir<0` and moves `x+dir*22`, so the fish always travels backwards. It also rotates to ±70° (`angle: dir*70`), which jaggies the pixels, and it is a generic grey fish unrelated to the 18 collection fish. → Flip the texture logic, draw 3 pre-rotated arc frames (rising, top, diving), and use one of the real pond or sea fish sprites at small size.
- **[C-24] Builder-placed ducks swim backwards too** (Med). In `IslandScene.moveIslanders()`, critters get `setFlipX(g.dx > 0)`, but `duck_0` faces right. → `setFlipX(g.dx < 0)` for directional critters (duck, and any future side-view animal).
- **[C-25] The swan is a box with a straight pole for a neck, and its tail is a black hole** (High). `swan_0`: the neck is a straight vertical 2 px column (the code comment says "long curved neck"). The head is 2x2 white with a 1 px eye, and the 2x2 orange bill floats with no outline. The tail is `R(2,7,3,4,OUT)`, a solid black 3x4 block that reads as a hole or a burnt tail. The body is a 13x5 box. At 20x16 the swan is barely bigger than the 14x11 duck. The swan is also missing from `art.html`'s Creatures tab, so nobody has reviewed it. → 28-32 px long, an S-curved 2 px neck, an outlined orange bill with a black knob, tail feathers raised in white with a grey shade, and folded wing feathers. Shot: [pond-summer-1-crop.png](shots/creatures/island/pond-summer-1-crop.png)
- **[C-26] Ducks and the swan float on the water like toys on a table: no waterline, no reflection, no wake** (High). The swan and duck bodies end in a hard black bottom outline sitting on top of the water. There are no submerged pixels, no light waterline row, and no reflection. The only water response is an occasional vector `ellipse` ring (`updateDucks`, every 18 ticks). → Cut the bottom 1-2 rows into a light waterline (`#d8f0ff` at 70%), tint the submerged part with the water colour, add a 2-3 px wobbling reflection below, and a pixel V-wake behind moving birds.
- **[C-27] Duck feet dangle below the hull, so the duck looks like it is standing on the water** (Med). In `duck_0/1` and `duckling_0/1`, orange 2x1 or 1x1 feet sit under the body (`R(3+f*2, 9, 2, 1)`), and flicking them is the duck's only animation. → Hide the feet under the waterline. Animate a 1 px body bob, a head turn, a "dabble" frame (tail up, head under) and a wing stretch. A mallard pair (green head, brown female) would be cozier than all-white Pekins.
- **[C-28] Duckling heads are black boxes with a yellow pixel for an eye** (Med). `duckling_*` draws `R(4,0,3,3,OUT)` then a single `R(5,1,1,1,'#f8d84a')`, so the head is outline with one yellow dot, and the duckling looks black-headed with no eye. → A 3x3 yellow head, a 1 px dark eye and an orange 2x1 bill.
- **[C-29] Ducklings pile up on their mother** (Med). In `updateDucks()`, each duckling reads `mom.trail[min(len-1, round(i*11/duckSp))]`, a spacing counted in frames, not distance. For the first seconds every duckling sits exactly on the mother (measured: all three at 212,546), and on a 120 Hz iPhone the spacing halves. → Space by distance along the path (for example every 9 px of arc length).
- **[C-30] The frog is a green TV with eyes, and its hop frame is a different object** (Med). `frog_0` is a black-outlined box with two 3x3 eye bumps, no pupils (cream 1 px dots), no legs, and a light 4x1 stripe for a mouth. `frog_1` (mid-hop) is a 4x3 green box on two 3x3 black legs, like a stool or the letter π, with no eyes. → A top-down or 3/4 frog with folded haunches, front feet, big eyes with dark pupils and a pale throat. Hop in 3 frames (crouch, stretched, land), with a splash sprite when it enters the pond.
- **[C-31] Lily pads are green bricks rotated to random angles, and they clash with the tile lily pads** (Med). `lilypad` is a 10x6 rectangle whose notch is a dark teal `#2a5a6a` block that reads as a hole. `Critters` does `setAngle(rnd(0,360))` and keeps rotating it, so the edges jaggy and shimmer. The same pond already has round tile lily pads with pink flowers. Shot: [pond-summer-1-crop.png](shots/creatures/island/pond-summer-1-crop.png) (three tilted green "books" next to the round pads). → Use the tile pad style: round pads with a V notch cut out (transparent, not teal), 3-4 pre-drawn orientations, drifting position only, no runtime rotation.
- **[C-32] The crab is a red box with handles** (Med). In `crab_0/1`, the claws are flat 3x2 orange rectangles with no outline and no pincer notch. The shell has no top outline (the fill starts on the outline row). The legs are four loose 1 px dots under the body, and the eyes are 1x2 black sticks. It reads as a space invader. → A wide oval carapace with a lighter top, outlined claws with a V notch held up, three 1 px diagonal legs per side touching the body, eye stalks with white tips, and a scuttle frame that moves the legs.
- **[C-33] Gulls read as black-and-white dominoes, and they are drawn at a non-integer scale** (Med). `gull_0/1` is mostly outline black: a 5x3 black body with a 3x1 white line, black 5x2 wing bars with a grey line, and a 2x1 bill beside the body with no head. `newGull()` draws it at `setScale(1.3)`, so pixel widths come out uneven. Shot: [winter-rain-contact.png](shots/creatures/island/winter-rain-contact.png) (winter-gull). → A white body, grey mantle, black wingtips, an M-shaped glide silhouette and a 3-frame flap, at 1x or 2x only.
- **[C-34] Butterflies are bow ties, or the letter Y when closed** (Med). In `fly_*` (8x6), the body is a 2x4 black bar and the wings are 3x3 squares with no outline. The closed frame is two 2x2 squares above the body, a "Y". → Forewing and hindwing shapes with a 1 px dark edge, antennae, and 3 frames (open, half, closed), with a few species patterns that match the Journal's insects.
- **[C-35] Fireflies are plus signs, not glows** (Low). `firefly` is a 3x3 `+` in `#f8ffb0` with ADD blending and no soft halo, so at night it reads as a cross or sparkle. Shot: [night-contact.png](shots/creatures/island/night-contact.png). → A 1-2 px body with a separate soft radial glow sprite (8-10 px, low alpha) that pulses, and a faint trail.

## 3. Ambient wildlife (`src/ambient.js`: `ambientTextures()`, `Ambient`)

- **[C-36] Rabbits have black heads, so the winter rabbit reads as a skunk or a goat** (High). `rabbit_0`: the head is `R(7,3,4,4,OUT)` with only 2x2 fur inside, and the ears are 2x5 outline bars, so the face is mostly black. `rabbit_2` (hop) has a 5x4 black head block. On the white `rabbit_w_*` this gives a white body with a black head. Shots: [crit-sheet3-10x.png](shots/creatures/crit-sheet3-10x.png), [winter-rain-contact.png](shots/creatures/island/winter-rain-contact.png). → Draw the head in fur colour with a 1 px outline, a 1 px eye with a catchlight, fur-coloured ears with pink insides, and a lighter muzzle. It should be the same design as the pet bunny (C-01).
- **[C-37] Sandpipers and sparrows are mostly outline, so they read as black birds or spiders** (Med). `piper_*`: the body is 5x4 black around 3x2 tan, the head 3x3 black around 1 tan pixel, and the legs are single black pixels (real sandpipers are pale grey-brown with white bellies). `sparrow_1` (pecking) is a black blob with a stick leg. `sparrow_fly_*` have unoutlined brown wing blocks hanging below the body. → Use the outline only on the outer edge, fill the head, show a white belly, and draw thin legs in a darker leg colour, not the outline colour.
- **[C-38] The squirrel's tail is a plank** (Med). `squirrel_0`: the tail is a 5x9 rectangle (the code comment says "tail curled") and reads as a door or a brick. In `squirrel_1` (running) the tail is a 5x5 box floating above. → A bushy S-curl tail with a lighter rim and a 2-frame flick, and ear tufts.
- **[C-39] The napping cat is a rectangle loaf that sleeps with its eyes open** (Med). `napcat_0/1` is a 13x7 box. Its "sleeping" eyes are 1 px dark dots (`R(11,3,1,1,OUT)`), which read as awake, and the tail is solid black on a ginger cat. `napcat_up` (sitting) is a box with a square head. Next to the pet cat it is half the size and a different style (C-01). → A curled crescent with the tail around its nose, closed eyes as 2 px curves (^ ^), ears that flick, and the same palette and outline as the pet cat.
- **[C-40] The sleeping "zzz" reads as "Iz"** (Low). In the ambient `zzz` texture, the small z is `R(0,5,3,1) R(1,6,1,1) R(0,7,3,1)`, a 3-1-3 "I" with no diagonal. There is also a separate `emote_zzz`, so there are two designs for the same thing. → Reuse one zzz emote everywhere and give the small z its diagonal.
- **[C-41] Snail and hermit crab shells are nested squares, like a target or a toaster** (Med). `snail_*` and `hermit_*`: the shell is a 6x6 or 7x6 box with a box inside and a pixel in the middle. The hermit crab's body is an unoutlined 3x2 red block, so the whole thing reads as a speech bubble or a toaster. Shot: [crit-sheet4-10x.png](shots/creatures/crit-sheet4-10x.png). → A round coiled shell with a 1 px spiral highlight. The hermit crab needs an outlined claw and legs coming out of the shell mouth.
- **[C-42] The sea turtle is a square board-game piece with loose flippers, rotated so it jaggies** (Med). `turtle_*`: the shell is a 10x10 box with three lighter rectangles. The flippers are unoutlined green 4x2 bars drawn under the shell, so only 2x2 squares stick out at the corners, not joined to anything. `Ambient.turtle()` uses `setAngle(atan2…)` at any angle, which breaks the pixels. Shot: [day-contact.png](shots/creatures/island/day-contact.png) (day-turtle). → An oval shell with a scute pattern and a pale rim, outlined flippers attached at the shoulders and hips, and 4 or 8 pre-drawn headings.
- **[C-43] The dragonfly has no outline and only two wings** (Low). `dragonfly_*`: an 8x1 blue line, a 3x3 head and two translucent wing blocks, the only unoutlined creature in the set. → Four wings in two pairs, a 1 px dark-outlined body with a segmented highlight, and a fast 2-frame wing shimmer.
- **[C-44] The bat's wings-up frame is a T, or a pistol** (Med). `bat_1` is a solid black horizontal bar with a stem under it. Neither frame shows ears, eyes or the scalloped edge of the wings, and pure black disappears against the night shade. Shot: [crit-sheet4-10x.png](shots/creatures/crit-sheet4-10x.png). → Scalloped wings with finger points, ear tips, a dark purple-brown body with a 1 px lighter rim, and 3 flap frames.
- **[C-45] Moths are 3x2 cream rectangles: placeholder** (Med). `moth` is `R(0,0,3,2,'#fff8e0')`, and 3 of them circle each lamp at night. They read as stray glitch pixels. Shot: [night-contact.png](shots/creatures/island/night-contact.png) (night-moth). → A 4x3 moth with two wing frames and a dusty tan-white body, lit by the lamp's glow rather than added on top.
- **[C-46] The owl is a carton with horns** (Low). `owl_*` is a 10x12 rectangle with 2x3 black tufts standing straight up from the top corners. → A rounded body, a lighter facial disc around the eyes, tufts angled outward, folded wings with a feather pattern, and a talon grip on the branch.
- **[C-47] Jellyfish are pink tables** (Low). `jelly_*` is a flat-topped 9x4 dome with four straight 1 px tentacles. → A rounded bell with a lighter rim and inner glow, and frilly, wavy tentacles in 3 frames.
- **[C-48] The sailboat has rectangular sails** (Low). `sailboat_*`: the main sail and jib are rectangles (`R(12,4,5+f,9)`, `R(5,7,5,7)`). → A triangular mainsail and jib with a light belly that fills in the wind.
- **[C-49] The dolphin has no visible eye, and its fluke is a comb** (Low). In the `dolphin` rows, the eye `e` maps to the outline colour right next to the outline, so it disappears. The tail fluke at x=0 is a vertical bar with notches. This is otherwise the best silhouette in the creature set. → A 1 px white-backed eye and a horizontal crescent fluke.
- **[C-50] The message bottle's cork is unoutlined and floats above the bottle, and the bottle is rotated on the water** (Low). `bottle`: the 2x2 cork sits above the outline, and in game it shows at an arbitrary angle with no waterline. → An outlined cork, a glass highlight, the lower third under a waterline, and a slow 2-frame bob instead of rotation.
- **[C-51] Three different bobbers and four different fish designs show up during fishing** (Med). The old fisherman's `bobber` (4x5, unoutlined, no outline at all) differs from the player's `bobberTexture()` (7x9, outlined), which differs from the Journal's CSS dots (`.bob-dot`, vector circles with a gradient). Fish come as the 8x4 jumping `fish`, the 26x16 collection fish, the fisherman's catch (the 8x4 fish at `setScale(1.3)`) and the flat blobs baked into the aquarium sprite (C-79). → One bobber sprite set (also used as the Journal icons) and one fish art source at two sizes.

## 4. Where creatures walk, swim and sit (behaviour that breaks the art)

- **[C-52] Wildlife walks on roofs, chimneys, signs, brick paving and cliff faces** (High). `IslandScene.ground_()` returns `'grass'` for any cell that isn't water, sand or `solid`, so paths, plazas, cliffs and the ground under buildings and props all count as meadow for `Ambient.onGrass()` and `spot()`. In game: a rabbit standing on top of the cottage chimney ([rabbit-on-chimney-3x.png](shots/creatures/fishing/rabbit-on-chimney-3x.png)), a rabbit hopping over a brick-paved patio, a squirrel sitting on a wooden sign whose label shows through, sparrows on the dirt cliff face, and a snail spawned on the cottage roof in the rain, hidden under the roof sprite ([day-contact.png](shots/creatures/island/day-contact.png), [winter-rain-contact.png](shots/creatures/island/winter-rain-contact.png)). → Give ambient spawning real ground classes (grass, path, plaza, cliff, roof, prop footprint), spawn and move only on true grass or meadow tiles, and keep 1 tile away from the footprints of buildings and props.
- **[C-53] Pixel art is rotated and squashed at runtime all over the outdoors** (Med). Lily pads spin continuously, the turtle rotates to any heading, the bottle sits at an arbitrary angle, the jumping fish rotates ±70°, dolphins tilt ±45°, the sailboat rocks ±3°, the fisherman's fish swings ±20°, the napping cat "breathes" with scaleY 1.04, frogs croak with scale 1.15/0.9, and squirrels twitch at 1.06. With `pixelArt: true` each of these shears, doubles or drops pixel rows, so the sprites shimmer. → Pre-drawn frames for every pose and heading, integer positions, and no runtime rotation or fractional scale on pixel sprites. Keep tweens for position and alpha only.
- **[C-54] Ripples, wakes and rain splashes are smooth vector ellipses** (Low). `ripple()`, the duck rings, frog plops and rain `splash()` are all `scene.add.ellipse(...).setStrokeStyle(1, …)` scaled to 2-2.4x, which draws anti-aliased rings with non-pixel strokes. → A 4-frame pixel ripple sprite (1 px ring growing 4→12 px) in the water-highlight colour.

## Systemic issues

- **Outline colour used as a fill.** Across pets, critters and ambient animals, legs, tails, heads and wings are drawn in `OUT` (`#1d1714`) instead of a darker fur or feather colour (rabbit heads, duckling heads, sandpiper bodies, gull bodies, pet legs, cat and dog tails, the swan's tail). Small animals then read as black blobs or black-headed. The fix for everything: a 1 px outline around the silhouette only, fills in the material's own shades.
- **Rectangles instead of shapes.** Squirrel tails, owl bodies, turtle shells, snail and hermit crab shells, sails, moths, lily pads and every weather particle are boxes. Nearly every ambient sprite is built from `fillRect` calls with no curves, which is why the set looks like programmer art next to the LPC-style characters and the Pokémon-style tiles.
- **Three art scales and styles for the same animals.** 32 px rounded-blob pets (`pets.js`), 8-16 px blocky wildlife (`ambient.js`, `critters.js`) and 12 px data icons (`collections`). A cat, a rabbit, a parrot or a fish is drawn two or three ways at sizes that don't match.
- **Palette swaps posing as different items.** About 40 of the 103 code-drawn items share a drawing with another item (coins, minerals, pottery, points, shells, meteorites, teeth, butterflies, flowers, galleons, 7+ fish).
- **Runtime rotation and fractional scales on pixel art.** Rotation (lily pads, turtle, bottle, jumping fish, dolphin, boat) and non-integer `setScale` (gull 1.3, fisherman's fish 1.3, catch 0.9, aquarium 0.7, shelf 0.8-0.89, viewer and journal stretching) are everywhere, so pixels shear and shimmer. Use pre-drawn frames and integer scales.
- **Nothing touches the water.** Water creatures (ducks, swan, bottle, jumping fish, tank fish) have no waterline, reflection or wake. The only water response is smooth vector ellipse rings.
- **Facing bugs from inverted flips.** The swan, the jumping fish and builder ducks move backwards because the sprite's facing direction and the `setFlipX` test disagree. Every directional sprite should face right by convention, with one shared helper (`faceTowards(dx)`).
- **Two-frame animation.** Nearly every creature has two frames, often 1 px apart. Walking, flapping, hopping and swimming need 3-4 frames to stop looking like sliding cut-outs.
