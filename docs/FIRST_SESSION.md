# Curation — First Session Walkthrough (draft)

Goal: ~10 minutes, one new idea at a time, the **HD painting reveal within 90 seconds**, and a reason to come back before the player closes the app.

## Principles
- Each step is under a minute.
- No ads, no gem shop, no sales pitches in the first session.
- Every action visibly improves the museum.
- First rewards are big; later ones drop off (see Mini-games rules).

## Step by step

**0. Loading screen**
The outside of the museum: boarded windows, weeds, a faded sign. "Loading…"

**1. Welcome (0:00–0:30)**
The **old caretaker** greets you (old, charming, loves the place even though it's fallen apart):
> "We're so excited you're the new owner of our town's museum! Back in the day we showed local artists, but lately we just can't compete with the big museums. It's been in disrepair for years. Really hoping you can breathe some new life into this place."

Name your museum. Quick character creator (skippable; editable later). The caretaker guides the rest of the tutorial.

**2. The dusty room (0:30–1:30)**
One small gray room, cracked tiles, dirty walls. Two paintings left behind, leaning against the wall: **The Harvesters** (Pieter Bruegel the Elder, 1565) and **The Horse Fair** (Rosa Bonheur, 1852–55). Both vaguely familiar but not most people's favorites; both CC0 at the Met.
- Learn to move (tap to walk, or hold and drag).
- Hang the first painting → **painting viewer reveal**: the camera turns to the wall and the HD painting appears. Swipe to the second painting.
- Prompt: rate it / add a note (optional).

**3. First mini-game: Mop Rush (1:30–3:00)**
"After hours" — clean the spills in 30 seconds. Tuned so the first run is an easy win with a big payout.

**4. Clean up (3:00–4:00)**
Spend coins to fix the floor and walls (instant). The room goes from gray to clean.

**5. Open the doors (4:00–5:00)**
First visitors walk in. Coins appear around the room → walk over to collect. Visitor counter appears ("12 visitors this month!").

**6. First crate (5:00–6:30)**
A free starter crate. Guaranteed van Gogh painting.
- Codex intro: "Vincent van Gogh 1/12."
- Rumor hint: "A red-bearded painter has been seen around town…" (famous visitor teaser).

**7. Decorate (6:30–7:30)**
Buy a first bench or plant → visitors go up. Hang the new painting.

**8. Daily rewards (7:30–8:30)**
Daily wheel spin (first spin rigged generous). Peek at the Gallery Shop.

**9. Curator's Office (8:30–10:00)**
The office is run down. Start its repair — the first construction, ~30 minutes.
- Message: "Your museum earns while you're away (up to 8 hours)."

## Reasons to come back
- Office repair finishes in ~30 min.
- Coins piling up (8h cap).
- Streak day 1; first-game-of-the-day bonus tomorrow.
- New guests every day.

## Days 2–3 (sketch)
- **Day 2:** streak bonus, a guest with a gift, unlock No Flash!, Curator's Office level 1 → second builder / first new room.
- **Day 3:** unlock Restoration, Gallery Shop fully open, first quest chain ("Collect 3 van Goghs").
