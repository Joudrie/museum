# Vision: the north star for Curation

In the user's own words: *a tycoon game with real substance to the humanities, a love
letter to life itself, to art of all kinds, ancient and modern, and most importantly fun.*

Check every feature against these:

1. **No wasted time.** Idle time still builds you up, but active play has real substance
   and real reasons to come back.
2. **Deep customization.** Players change shape and architecture, not just colors and materials.
3. **Real art is the core.** Real public-domain paintings and objects, shown in HD against the pixel world.
4. **People.** Pokémon-style talking guests. Meet famous artists and philosophers.
5. **No limits on scope.** Inspired by Boston's museums: art, archaeology, natural history,
   science, aquarium and more.
6. **Rabbit holes.** Every piece should make people want to learn more, whether it's
   dinosaur bones or Dutch painting. Short, true, curious facts.
7. **Earned, not bought.** Collections come from active, hands-on play built around
   discovery and preservation: dig sites to excavate bones, catch every aquarium fish
   yourself, restore paintings. No throwaway mini-games.
8. **Fun first.**
