# Artist brief: Curation (draft)

For a hired pixel artist or small studio. Read `docs/ART-BIBLE.md` for the rules and `docs/ART-AUDIT.md` for what exists.

## The game in three sentences
*Curation* is a cozy pixel-art museum tycoon for iPhone (portrait, browser). You collect real public-domain art, shown in HD on the wall, restore it, display it, and sail to islands to find more. Visitors talk like Pokémon characters, famous artists drop in, and every object has a short true story.

## What we want
One cohesive style that replaces ~25 mixed free packs and the placeholder art drawn in code. We **own** everything delivered.

## Scope and phases
Each phase ends with a delivery we review before the next starts. We would sign off per phase and pay per phase.

| # | Phase | Deliverable | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Test (paid)** | Style tile: one room corner (floor, two walls, window, chair in 4 facings, plant, a framed painting, one character, a scale sheet) | Decides the hire. About 1 week |
| 1 | **Core kit** | Master palette, wall and floor kit (3 styles), 20 furniture and display pieces in 4 facings, 8 frames | The building blocks |
| 2 | **Characters** | 3 body builds, walk and idle, layered hair / outfit / hat / accessory, 6 famous people with busts | Reuses our parts list |
| 3 | **Terrain** | Autotile for 8 materials, cliffs, water, animated loops, island props | Matches the corner scheme |
| 4 | **Museum exterior and shaped rooms** | Facade kit in 3 styles, day / dusk / night, seasonal props, glass / curved / domed wall segments | |
| 5 | **Expedition sets** | Dig site, shipwreck, tomb, badlands, ice cave tiles and props; restoration studio props with before / after stages | For pillar 4 |
| 6 | **UI and effects** | Icons, 9-patch panels, emotes, effects | |

The first four phases are the minimum for a game that looks like one thing. Phases 5 and 6 can be split out or done later.

## What the artist gets
- The repo's current art (screenshots at 390×844) to see what is being replaced.
- Real examples of the 32px grid, the fake-3D room, and the island tileset.
- A **placeholder-to-final mapping sheet**: every current object name and the family it belongs to, so nothing is forgotten.
- Quick answers (a single point of contact) and a review within two working days.

## What we need back
Everything in "Technical delivery" in the bible, plus:
- layered sources,
- a signed **work-for-hire / full buyout** (no credit-required, no share-alike, no non-commercial limits),
- confirmation that no AI-generated art or copied third-party assets are used (a disclosure, not a promise to avoid reference),
- the right for us to modify and recolour freely.

## How we judge it
The review checklist at the end of the bible, plus the **gap test**: build the entrance, the main hall and a vertical, a horizontal and a big hall in every size from the kit, and look for black pixels between any two wall planes.

## Budget and timing
Not decided; the owner sets these. For planning: phase 0 is a small paid test, phases 1–4 are the main commission (months 2–6 of the plan), phases 5–6 follow. We should get quotes per phase, not one lump sum, and for at least two artists / studios.

## Where to look for artists
Candidates are chosen by the owner. Useful places: itch.io creators who sell tilesets, OpenGameArt contributors already credited in `CREDITS.md` (Sharm, bluecarrot16, Death's Darling, AntumDeluge; they know the grid, and a re-licensed or commissioned extension of LPC is an option), Pixel Joint, Twitter / Bluesky pixel-art tags, ArtStation, and small studios on Fiverr Pro or Upwork. Ask for portfolios with **interiors and tilesets**, not just characters.
